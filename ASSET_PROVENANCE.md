```text

Asset type: wide hero illustration for a trustworthy German mental-health self-assessment landing page
Primary request: Create a calm, welcoming editorial illustration that helps an adult visitor feel understood and safe while considering a self-reflection questionnaire.
Scene/backdrop: airy abstract interior with soft organic shapes, subtle plants and a quiet window-like light area
Subject: one gender-neutral adult person seated comfortably with a warm drink and a simple notebook, relaxed and thoughtful rather than sad; a few gentle abstract shapes suggest different ways of thinking and sensory perception
Style/medium: premium modern editorial illustration, softly textured gouache and clean geometric forms, sophisticated healthcare brand aesthetic
Composition/framing: wide 16:10 landscape, subject on the right half with generous negative space on the left, suitable for responsive web cropping
Lighting/mood: soft daylight, reassuring, grounded, humane, non-clinical
Color palette: deep forest green, sage, warm cream, muted terracotta and small golden accents, matching an elegant accessible website
Constraints: no text, no logos, no medical symbols, no puzzle pieces, no infantilization, no stereotypes about autism, no diagnostic imagery, diverse and inclusive presentation
```
